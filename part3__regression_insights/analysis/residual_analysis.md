Residual Analysis

Residual calculation
Residual = Actual Sales − Predicted Sales

Positive residuals
Large positive residuals indicate stores that are outperforming model predictions.
Possible causes:

Strong local demand

Effective promotions

Competent store management

Higher customer loyalty

Negative residuals
Large negative residuals indicate stores that are underperforming relative to predictions.
Possible causes:

Weak local demand

Inventory shortfalls

Nearby strong competitors

Operational inefficiencies

Business insights

The final regression model accounts for 82.29% of the variation in sales.

Residual analysis shows that, despite strong overall performance, some store-level effects are not captured by the model.

Potential missing variables include local competition, seasonal demand, regional promotions, and store management quality.

Use the model as a decision-support tool rather than treating it as a perfect predictor.
